A free AI voice assistant for Windows

ORRIN is a voice assistant that runs on your own Windows PC. Say "Hey Orrin", then what you want: open apps, control music and volume, set timers, ask questions. The AI runs locally on your graphics card or processor, so there are no API keys, no account and no subscription.

It is made for gamers and tech fans, and it stays quiet while you play.

Features

  • PersonalitiesMake it sarcastic, calm or hyped up. Say "be more sarcastic" and it is.
  • Protocol colour effectsSwitch its whole look and lighting with one voice command.
  • Levels and achievementsEarn XP as you use it and unlock new colours, voices and personalities.
  • Song makerDescribe a song and it makes one with vocals, right on your PC (NVIDIA card needed).
  • Local AI, no API keysChat, answers and voice run on your own hardware. Nothing to sign up for.

FAQ

Is ORRIN free?

Yes. ORRIN is free to download and use. On itch.io it is pay what you want, so you can pay $0 or leave a tip. There is no subscription.

Do I need an account or API keys?

No. There is no ORRIN account and no API keys. The AI models run on your own PC, and they are free.

Does it work offline?

Mostly. Voice commands and the AI run on your PC. ORRIN only goes online to download the AI models you approve, to search the web when you ask it to, and to check for updates.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a 4-core processor, 8 GB of RAM, about 8 GB of free disk, and a microphone. No graphics card is needed for the basics. Pictures and the song maker need an NVIDIA card with 4 GB or more.

Is my voice private?

Yes. Your voice, chats and files stay on your PC. ORRIN has no telemetry and never uses your camera.
